## Understanding Your Options

Kenya is served by a network of domestic and international carriers operating out of Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) in Nairobi, Moi International Airport in Mombasa, Kisumu International Airport, Eldoret International Airport, and Wilson Airport, which handles scheduled regional and charter services. Whether you are flying between Nairobi and Mombasa or connecting internationally, comparing your options before booking helps you find the best balance of price, schedule, and comfort.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far in advance should I book?

For domestic flights within Kenya, booking 2–6 weeks ahead generally secures the best fares. For international flights, 2–4 months is a good rule of thumb.

### What is the difference between Wilson Airport and JKIA?

Wilson Airport mainly handles regional and domestic scheduled and charter flights to destinations such as the Masai Mara and Lamu, while JKIA is Kenya's main hub for domestic and international scheduled services.

### Do flight prices change a lot?

Yes. Airfares are dynamic and respond to demand, season, and how close the departure is. Comparing options and booking at the right time saves real money.
